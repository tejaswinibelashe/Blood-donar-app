About Us

Company Intro

BloodLink is a leading digital health platform dedicated to connecting blood donors with patients and hospitals in real-time. Since our inception, we have built a robust ecosystem that simplifies the blood donation and request process.

Mission

To ensure that nobody loses their life due to a shortage of blood by creating a seamless, accessible, and fast blood donation network.

Vision

A world where every community is self-sufficient in its blood supply, fostering a culture of regular voluntary blood donation.

Our Services
  • Emergency Blood Request Matching
  • Donor Scheduling and Alerts
  • Hospital Inventory Management
  • Community Blood Drive Organizing
