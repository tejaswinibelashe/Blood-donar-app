About BloodLink

BloodLink is a revolutionary platform designed to bridge the gap between blood donors and those in critical need.

Our Mission

To eliminate blood shortages in emergencies by providing a real-time, location-based network connecting hospitals, patients, and willing donors.

Why we started

Founded in 2026, we recognized that millions of lives are lost simply because blood isn't available exactly when and where it's needed. Traditional blood banks are often disconnected from immediate emergency situations.


How it Works

  • Real-time Sync: Integrates seamlessly across Mobile and Web applications.
  • Location Based: Finds the absolute closest donors to minimize wait times.
  • Verified Hospitals: Works directly with trusted local hospitals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can donate blood?

Anyone aged 18-65, weighing at least 50kg, and in good health can donate.

Is the data synced with the mobile app?

Yes! The web application and Android application share the exact same real-time database.

How quickly are emergency requests broadcasted?

Instantly. As soon as you hit submit, all active donors in your vicinity are notified.
